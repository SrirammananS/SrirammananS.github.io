What is VAPT?

VAPT stands for Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing. It's a comprehensive two-step cybersecurity process used to identify and address security weaknesses in systems, networks, and applications.

1. Vulnerability Assessment (VA)

Automated scanning and identification of known vulnerabilities in systems, networks, and applications using specialized tools and techniques.

2. Penetration Testing (PT)

Simulating real-world cyber attacks to exploit identified vulnerabilities and assess their actual impact on business operations.
